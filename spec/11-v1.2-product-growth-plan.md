# v1.2.0 Product Growth Plan — final approved direction

## Product premise
OWL1000 remains one continuous 1000-day creator journey, but the first visible goal is intentionally small enough to start: 100 days. Users do not restart separate challenges at each milestone.

## Milestones
- 100 days → 100-day badge → next target 300
- 300 days → 300-day badge → next target 600
- 600 days → 600-day badge → next target 900
- 900 days → 900-day badge → next target 1000
- 1000 days → OWL1000 completion badge + completion experience

A milestone badge is earned after the milestone day has been reached and the participant records a qualifying weekly success. Earned badges are permanent.

**Sequential rule:** one successful weekly proof can award at most the participant's next unearned milestone. A participant returning after a long inactive period cannot unlock several badges with one submission.

## Creator identity levels
Users are creators from the moment they start. Level names represent growth in confidence and craft, not permission to call oneself a creator.

1. Lv.1 **크리에이터 / Creator** — from challenge start until the 100-day badge is earned
2. Lv.2 **루틴 크리에이터 / Routine Creator** — after 100 badge
3. Lv.3 **스토리 크리에이터 / Story Creator** — after 300 badge
4. Lv.4 **시그니처 크리에이터 / Signature Creator** — after 600 badge
5. Lv.5 **마스터 크리에이터 / Master Creator** — after 900 badge

Day 1000 is not a sixth owl level. It is the final OWL1000 completion state layered on top of Lv.5.

## Failure philosophy
Creator level is permanent progress and never regresses because of weekly failures. Failure affects current streak / recent condition / weekly status, not identity already earned. The old egg reset / stage-drop visual model is removed from participant UI.

## Mascot direction
The approved concept uses one consistent warm floral owl character that matures across five stages. Stage 1 is already a complete small owl; there is no egg stage.

- Lv.1: smallest, simple silhouette, curious/confident beginner energy.
- Lv.2: slightly larger, notebook/pencil and routine cues.
- Lv.3: expressive storytelling/book cues and richer feathers.
- Lv.4: refined personal identity and signature details.
- Lv.5: iconic premium hero form with the richest floral treatment.

## Screen direction
### Landing
> 당신은 이미 창작자입니다.  
> 먼저 100일. 매주 한 편씩.  
> 1000일 동안 당신만의 세계를 만들어가세요.

### Dashboard
The primary progress module shows the current milestone. Overall 1000-day progress remains secondary context. If the threshold is already reached but the badge is waiting for the next success, the bar stays at 100% and clearly explains the pending verification condition.

### Level-up celebration
After a successful submission awards a milestone, show a small non-blocking celebration card with the new owl/level and badge. It appears once for that redirect, cleans its query state so refresh does not replay it, and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Community
Cards show creator level, compact owl thumbnail, current streak and milestone badges without exposing private account data.

## Data model
`challenge_badges` is the immutable milestone history/source-of-truth. Current target and creator level are derived from earned badges and are not duplicated as mutable columns.
